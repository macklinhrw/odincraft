# A first year: what a player expects, written before it was played

Written on 2026-11-17 before any of `year-*` was run, as the way of working set after P3 asks
(`open-decisions.md`, 2026-11-16). The player is one who knows Dwarf Fortress and RimWorld and has
never seen this game. Each moment is judged against its rows; what was found is in `plan.md`,
"A first year, as played".

## Before the landing

| Moment | The player expects |
|---|---|
| The front door | Something to look at at once, one obvious way in, and the name of what it leads to. No wait with nothing said. |
| The world's map | To see where the islands are, to take one and read what it is in rows, and to be led from it to the next thing with one button. Nothing on it overlapping or cut. |
| Planning | A place already offered so that Enter is enough; what the place has and lacks; who is in the boat. To be warned, not stopped. |
| Setting out | To see the boat and the party at once, with the view already on them, the clock running and the first thing to do said once. |

## The first day (RimWorld's first hour; DF's first season)

| Moment | The player expects |
|---|---|
| Ashore | The party stands together by its goods; the stores say what it has; an alert says what is wanting, shortly, and goes when it is answered. |
| The first orders | Stockpile first, then wood, then a room: each drag shows what it will mark; the dwarves go at once; what is cut lies where it fell and is carried in without being asked. The order of things is the player's, and nothing ordered is silently left. |
| A room in the hill | The dig is seen through the cut; the stone comes out and is stored; nobody is shut in; a wall and a roof close it. |
| Evening | Those who are thirsty or hungry see to it themselves and come back to their work. The alerts at the left are few and each is true. |
| The first night | The picture is darker but everything can still be read and ordered. Sleepers lie in the sleeping place, each in a place of its own, and a sleeper is plainly asleep (not a corpse). A fire is a light. The idle do not stand in one knot. |
| Dawn | One line says the day. The messages of the night have gone; none lie over another. |

## The year

| Moment | The player expects |
|---|---|
| The first rain (evening of the 4th of spring) | Rain is seen falling outdoors and not in a room that is cut open. The weather's line says it. Dwarves indoors are dry; a rack in the open stops and says so. |
| Late spring | Food is being dried and stored; the stores tell kept from fresh; a stockpile that is full says so in so many words, where the player looks for it. |
| Midsummer | The lean season is said ahead and then, while it lasts, not as a standing wall of alerts. The fire by the racks burns while timber lies in the hold. Work that cannot be reached is said once and costs nothing. |
| The first cold (autumn) | A warning before anybody is in danger, with what to do. A dwarf that is cold goes to the fire or indoors by itself. |
| The worst of winter | Indoors by the fire the seven are warm and fed from what was kept. The fire is fed from the timber stockpile without an order. Firelight stays in the room. The night can be read. |
| The next spring | Seven alive. The stores, the alerts and the list of dwarves are as readable as on the first day: no card stuck open, no message a season old, no alert that was true once. |

## Throughout

- Nothing on the screen overlaps, is cut off, or says a thing that is not so.
- Whatever is selected is plainly selected.
- What dwarves do unasked is what a player would have asked for: carry in, feed the fire, sleep in the sleeping place, eat what spoils first.
- A year costs no more to run than it did.
