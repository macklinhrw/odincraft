# A new world whose mainland lies within reach of its start, made and watched through the mainland's
# stage, played from the player's seat (step N1, second part; open-decisions.md, 2026-11-17): seed 1,
# whose nearest large land is 51 km from the start. Its coarse stage takes about half a minute the
# first time and is kept; a second playing reads it back and the stage passes at once, so to watch the
# stage again remove what was kept first:
#   rm -f build/worlds/landmass-1-*
#   build/odincraft --door --play=docs/interesting-world/real/play/n1-new.txt
# The pictures go to out/play/. The pointer's figures are pixels of a picture 2560 by 1440.

print 1. a new world of seed 1
part Make a new world
type 1
wait 0.2
key Enter
wait 0.8

print 2. the mainland's stage: its steps counted and the time left said, while the map is looked at
until mainland 120
wait 4
snap out/play/n1-01-stage.png
say
print the mainland in the hand while it is formed: its card says so, with how far it has come
part isles.mainland
wait 0.6
snap out/play/n1-02-stage-card.png
say

print 3. the world can be entered: the mainland is drawn from its 64 m cells, rivers and all
until ready 300
part All islands
wait 0.6
shot out/play/n1-03-ready.png
say
part isles.mainland
wait 0.6
part As land
shot out/play/n1-04-mainland.png
say
part Ground to walk on
shot out/play/n1-05-mainland-ground.png

print 4. a large land farther off: known, not formed, why, and what forming it would take
part The west land
wait 0.6
part As land
shot out/play/n1-06-west-land.png
say

print 5. the mainland's coast looked at near: the valleys of its 64 m cells
view over=75000,-8000 span=70000
wait 0.6
shot out/play/n1-07-coast.png
