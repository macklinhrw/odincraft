Interface wireframes

Drawings for interface.md, not the game. They show where each thing lands on the screen and why. The land behind the first screen is the default island's map; the view from above does not exist in the game yet. Names and numbers are examples.

1. The play screen

Four regions, each with one job. The top is what is always true. The lower left is the hand: what is selected, or the tool in use, with the orders under it. The right is what to know. The middle is the world, and no panel covers it.

  1. The hold and the clock. Where and when, first thing the eye meets. Pause and speeds live with the clock, with their keys.
  2. The seven. One picture each. A mark (a shape, not only a colour) when one is hurt, hungry or idle. Click selects; click again goes there.
  3. The stores, in a few words. Only what the hold is living on now. Click opens the full list.
  4. Messages. What just happened. They fade, and are kept in the chronicle.
  5. Alerts. What is wrong and still true, worst first. Each goes to its cause.
  6. How the world is seen. The cut's level, lenses (rock, water in the ground, rain), and the walk into first person.
  7. The hand. The selected thing: what it is, what it is doing and why, then tabs, then what can be done. A tool's choices appear here instead when a tool is held.
  8. Orders. Things to do, left. Each shows its key. Next to the hand, so an order and its target are a short reach apart.
  9. Lists. Things to know, right. A list opens down the right side, a third of the screen, with the world still in view.
  10. The world. What will be marked shows before the button is let go.
