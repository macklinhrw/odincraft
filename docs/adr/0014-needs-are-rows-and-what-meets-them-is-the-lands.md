# Needs are rows, the day is read from the tick, and what meets a need is the land's

A dwarf gets thirsty, hungry and tired, and sees to itself (`needs.odin`). The world has a day and a year (`clock.odin`). What a dwarf drinks and eats is what the island has (`food.odin`). This is the first half of step P4; shelter and warmth are its second.

It is done as the games it is measured by do it, read from their code and data (`interesting-world/notes/colony-sim-reference.md`, section 14).

- **The date is a function of the seconds since the boat stood off** (`date_at`): a day is 1,200 of them, the year 48 days in four seasons of twelve, and the boat stands off at 08:00 on the first day of spring. The dwarves' world reads it from its own tick (`date_tick`) and the screen from the same count, so the day the screen says is the day the dwarves live by, and no clock is shared between the threads that was not already (ADR 0008, ADR 0009). Each day of the game stands for 7.6 of the sun's year: the sun's height, and what grows and swims, are read on that day (`Date.sun`). The weather's spells are read by the game's own days, so one lasts as many of them as it did of the sun's.
- **A need is a row of `WANTS`**: how much of the whole it falls in a day awake and asleep, under what a dwarf with nothing in hand sees to it, under what it leaves its work for it, how long the act takes, and how many days a dwarf lasts with nothing. Each dwarf has a pace of its own at each, drawn from who it is. A later need (warmth) is a row and the procedure that meets it.
- **Needs come before work, and work is not taken up between them.** An idle dwarf looks to its needs before it looks for work (`care_look`); one that has finished a block looks to them before it takes the next (`care_calls`); and a need that will not wait takes it from its work (`needs_tick`). The work that is itself the remedy, gathering and fishing for a hungry dwarf, is not left for hunger: played, a starving party dropped every catch three seconds in and starved beside the stream.
- **What meets a need is found in the land, by the rules that made it.** A dwarf drinks standing in fresh water: a floor the land wets, over the sea's level, in or beside a land cell that `fresh` holds on, which is a stream that runs all year, a lake or a pond. It eats a thing that lies in a cell (ADR 0012). It sleeps on dry ground where it is.
- **A food is a row of `FOODS`** that names a ground plant, a tree or a fish the world already has, and adds what a person takes of it and when. A column bears where the generator stands that plant, tree or fish in it (`plant_at`, `trees_standing`, the stream's fish as `creatures_make` put them) and the row's season holds. One rule says what a box bears (`bearing_of`); the hold asks it of its own blocks and the frame of the blocks it draws, as with every order (ADR 0011).
- **Gather and Fish are marks like any** (`WORKS`): dragged, taken by the dwarves themselves, done once. A plant or tree taken bears again next year, and fished water holds fish again in four days: the hold keeps the day each column was taken (`Hold.picked`), and the plants are left standing, since it is their shoots and nuts that were taken.
- **A hungry dwarf with no food lying anywhere marks the nearest for itself** (`forage`), within a short walk, as much as a meal for those who are hungry, and takes those marks before other work. RimWorld's pawn eats from a bush; Dwarf Fortress's dwarf hunts vermin. The marks are the hold's and are shown, so the player sees a party living from hand to mouth, and an alert says what to do about it.
- **Death comes by stages that are said.** At nothing the log and the screen say so and how long is left; at half the time left they say it again; then the dwarf dies, which is said with where and when, and an alert stands until it is looked at. The days spent with nothing are made good as slowly as they were lost. The body lies where it fell, takes up no floor, and comes down with the ground under it.
- **The log has one line a day for what is routine**: at each dawn what the party drank and ate the day before and how long the stored food lasts. A line for every drink would be a thousand lines a season.

We chose food as rows on the world's own plants and fish over a list of foods with places drawn for them, because what lives on an island is never a preference: on the start island of seed 7 no ayu swim, since none reached it, and the hold says so. We chose marks done once over standing zones for gathering and fishing: a zone is a second kind of order with its own rules, and the season already makes the player come back. We chose to let a dwarf that finds no food forage for itself over letting it starve beside a wood of shoots: a hold that needs an order for every meal is not one that looks after itself.

## Consequences

- **The dwarves' world stays deterministic** (ADR 0009). The date is the tick's; water, plants, trees and fish are the generator's; where the nearest of several is chosen, ties are broken by the cell. The test plays gather, fish and a food stockpile into the second day three ways and compares the logs.
- **Nothing counts on seven.** The dead keep their place among the hold's dwarves, so that who is who does not change; whatever acts or counts skips them (`ashore`, `living`, `hold_living`).
- **A thing that is eaten is a material that is no block** (`.Food`; `BLOCK_MATS` are those the atlas has tiles for). It lies, is carried and is stored as stone is; a heap of it is 24 and an armful 8. A stockpile takes everything, stone, timber or food.
- What the frame knows is in `Shown`: each dwarf's needs, what it lacks, whether it is seeing to itself, and of a dead one what it died of and when; the columns taken; how long the stored food lasts; and whether the hold is foraging.
- **Night is lit as by a full moon**, far brighter than a true one (`sky_ambient`, `shaders.metal`): a hold is kept through the night, and the player must see it.
- `--hour` and `--day` hold the sun where they say, for a picture of the land. The date the screen says is still the clock's.
- **Left for later**: shelter and warmth, and weather that acts (P4's second half); beds; food that spoils, and cooking, drying and brewing (P5); farming; hunting; provisions an expedition brings (E-1 with P5); a dwarf's mood at hunger and at a death (P8); burial.
