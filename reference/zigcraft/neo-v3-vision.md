This section is normative. Every phase, every gate, and every architectural argument must be resolvable against it. **The project is nothing without its vision.**

We are building a **world**: a massive, living, breathing place in the spirit of [[Dwarf Fortress]]'s world generation — a place where we will create our dreams. Concretely:

- A Minecraft-style voxel game whose stable front door is a **Home screen** with Continue, Worlds, New World, and Quit, and whose **first act in a new world is mandatory world creation**. New World starts with a fresh visible seed and distinct save identity by default; explicit reuse of the same complete inputs remains deliberately reproducible and never overwrites another world. The player chooses a seed, finite world, and detail profile; watches truthful bounded stage/work progress; studies the completed atlas; selects an expedition site; and only then enters first person. In play, a real pause menu owns Resume, Save, Save & Return Home, and Save & Quit without silently dropping dirty state. World creation, expedition selection, persistence, and return to Home are part of the game, not an engine previewer or optional debug step.
- A **massive finite world with a persisted causal atlas**, not an infinite coordinate function discovered during play. The atlas owns landmasses, oceans, geologic history and strata, climate, basins, rivers, lakes, soils, ecological regions, mineralization, aquifers, major underground structures, habitats, and expedition-site facts. Generation automatically saves this immutable world root so it can be closed and loaded again. Play uses a separate durable world save layered over that root; it owns the expedition and, as those systems arrive, discovery, resource depletion, ecological change, sparse block changes, entities, and simulation state without rewriting or duplicating generated truth. Fine voxels are realized from the root plus saved state around selected and visited places; the entire world is never rasterized at one-block resolution.
- **Realistic terrain** in the class of [[TerraForged]] / ReTerraForged (as used in packs like GregTech: New Horizons), with our own improvements: continents shaped by geological history, rivers that exist because water drains downhill through connected basins, valleys and floodplains formed with erosion and deposition, and biomes that follow climate and landform. A river is a terrain system with a bed, banks, water surface, valley, discharge, and downstream identity — never a thin blue line painted on top. Realism comes from causal structure, not from naming more noise layers.
- A **causally living world**, not disconnected content painted over terrain. Geologic events produce strata, structures, aquifers, caves, and mineral deposits; climate, water, parent material, relief, organisms, disturbance, and time produce soils and ecological communities; plants and animals occupy coherent habitats and ranges; later settlements and history arise from the same geography and resources. Biome, resource, and underground-region names summarize that truth; they never replace it.
- **Natural truth first; explicit game design second.** Each seed and recipe produces exactly one accepted world, never a secretly rerolled favorite. A versioned, inspectable design pass guarantees a dispersed portfolio of viable but meaningfully different expedition choices, legible risks, and regional access to essential opportunities while preserving local scarcity, danger, discovery, trade, and surprise. It may simplify or exaggerate reality for beauty and playability, but it may not paste pity resources, fauna, or terrain beneath the player after site selection.
- **Bounded detail profiles.** Limited generation must complete within 4 minutes on the reference host; Detailed within 10 minutes; Deep within 15 minutes. Each profile runs a fixed deterministic recipe and refines the same protected macro world without changing parent resource wealth, ecological capacity, or history. A future 25–30 minute profile may add deeper geological, ecological, and world history, but it is not part of the present foundation.
- **View distance to the horizon.** Standing on a mountain, you see terrain the way you would in real life — ridgelines, river valleys winding to the coast, kilometers of world — and you can walk to all of it without a loading hitch or hidden macro-world regeneration.
- **Smooth and highly optimized.** World creation runs off the frame thread with responsive progress and cancellation; play never performs global geology or hydrology. No main-thread hitches, ever. Optimization exists to buy the world, view distance, and realism; it has no value the player cannot feel.

The hard demo that defines "foundation complete": *launch at Home; create a new Limited world within its four-minute cap while its real progress remains visible; inspect its atlas, compare several viable and strategically different expedition sites by water, climate, ecology, geological/resource potential, and hazards, and choose one near a river; then fly and walk from mountains through a carved river valley to the coast, with far terrain visible for kilometers, stable near/far transitions, and no hitches; break and place blocks, use the pause menu to save and return Home, quit, restart, Continue, and return to the same world state — playable by a human, in a window, today.*

If a piece of work does not move that demo forward, it is outside the foundation.
