## Game Inspiration
- **Dwarf Fortress** + Procedural World Generation (Minecraft Inspiration)
- AI (using OpenRouter) Agents, Generation of Background
- RimWorld

## Ideas
- Gameplay
	- Top-down view like RimWorld with the depth of Dwarf Fortress's camera and world system
	- The world can stay voxel based, have height, and use my procedural world generation
	- There could be a main top-down camera view, as well as first person view possible in the future
- Replayability (and Playability)
	- Use AI models from API along with a series of frameworks for generating a simulated world with depth and AI agents
