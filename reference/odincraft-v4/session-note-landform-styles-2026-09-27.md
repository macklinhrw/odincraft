On 2026-09-27 the owner said the world is uninteresting and asked to start executing a new direction: keep the macro simulation (crust, 1 km mesh, drainage, climate, geology, ecology) as the "why", and generate the sub-kilometre surface from designed landform styles chosen by that context, instead of per-place physical transactions carved into a finished heightfield (the 200-type ledger and waterfall transactions were effectively paused).

**Why:** walking-scale terrain came only from one generic noise vocabulary; carved places read as excavations; 0 of 200 crafted types were ever accepted.

**How to apply:** The runtime lab layer is `src/terrain/relief_style.odin` (styles off/auto/layered/knobs), hooked into `model_sample` and `model_region` in `query.odin`; `--landform=` game flag; tooling in `experiments/landform/` (see its README). Styles are raise-only so saved channels/lakes stay valid. Pitfalls found: `m.lake_cells` marks lake *ownership* (505k cells in seed 555), not wet ground; features stamped on a lattice and rejected near water vanish in lake districts — make them follow drainage (fade by water distance) instead; dome profiles need tangential feet or they end in soil walls; cliffs need bare-rock soil stripping or trees/soil hide them. Same day, owner asked for natural woodland borders (square 512 m lattice patches in `local_habitat_profile`): `src/terrain/habitat_mosaic.odin` adds `--habitats=natural` (warped two-scale stand cells, 32 m blended corners with thread-local cache, stochastic ecotone, riparian bands from `style_water_grid` with flow ≥ 0.5). A single thresholded smooth field makes ringed bands — use independent stand cells. Rivers/lakes (same day): `river_planform.odin` `--rivers=natural` rebuilds D8 reaches (smooth + wander + meanders, pinned junctions), adds `Channel_Node.bend` point bars, shore sand/gravel, reeds, lake-bed deepening and depth-shaded water; `--lab=auto+natural+rivers` combines all. Lake levels and block tops are whole metres, so shallow water is exactly 1 m — depth thresholds must allow ≥1. Lakes were 1–3 m deep flat sheets. River fit (same day): stages carved downstream-min to ground−bank with Kahn junction order; lake cells are a floor, not a pin (pinning cut ravines through hillside parts of 256 m lake cells). Pitfalls: fills must return a floor not max(base,fill) (that undid every valley cut); fill from the nearest reach only (upstream reaches build walls); ranging over a missing map entry segfaults; `lake_cells` edges make water walls — handled by `lake_containment` rims. Use `lab walls` (walls + water steps) as the regression metric. Floating-river lessons: never clamp water per column (drapes water over slopes); fit stages on a dense 6 m ground profile with adaptive node insertion (sparse nodes float over gullies); valley-seek the centreline sideways; always floor river stage at a lake's level through its water (arrival-only rule or draining lake cells both made >1000 walls). Game runs without lab flags unless `--lab=` is passed — check `ps` before assuming. Performance (owner hit multi-minute opens and broken streaming): stale region results are discarded and restarted, so slow region builds livelock streaming — keep lab region cost low; game now uses `model_region_parallel` (identical output). Rebuilt rivers cache to `seed-N.ocp.labrivers` (store only position/water/flow/bend/lake + per-run gentle; river_shape recomputes the rest so cached == fresh). Owner found knobs too frequent: now clustered (~20% of granite), 5–25 m. Don't run heavy 12-thread lab jobs while the owner is playing. Sept 27: owner made lab options default — revision ≥15 worlds get auto+natural+rivers unless any --lab/--landform/--habitats/--rivers flag is given (`lab_explicit`); older saves keep recipe (block edits). Creation worker runs lab_configure after save (≈27 s cold, cache .labrivers). Near-water depth shading must end at the distant water colour or the resident square shows. Seed 555 (owner's world, saved spot 167601,-160898) is granite lowland; steep bedded test site is 377601,-190898. Not committed — owner keeps diffs uncommitted. Related: [[relief-revision-nine]].
