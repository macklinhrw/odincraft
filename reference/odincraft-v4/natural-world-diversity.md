# Natural-world diversity and the long-term fantasy direction

Owner requirements, September 6, 2026. This supplements the foundation and reconstruction plans. It is an implementation/acceptance requirement, not a declaration that the current game meets it.

The immediate target is a place worth hiking through. A biome must contain varied local experiences: enclosed woodland, water, exposed rock, changing valley shape, elevated views and quiet stretches between landmarks. Different biome colors alone do not satisfy diversity. The owner specifically references Jūniko in Japan, waterfalls, distinctive rock formations, cliffs and valleys, and still has not encountered convincing mountains during ordinary exploration.

Jūniko is a reference for connected landscape experience, not a map to reproduce. Japan's Ministry of the Environment describes 33 lakes and marshes, thought to have formed through an early-eighteenth-century earthquake and subsequent landslide. This points to basin-forming events and disrupted drainage as a missing process family; adding more river strips will not create that landscape. [Ministry of the Environment](https://www.env.go.jp/nature/isan/worldheritage/en/shirakami/area/a06.html). The Forestry Agency also describes blocked valleys and ground collapse. [Forestry Agency](https://www.rinya.maff.go.jp/tohoku/introduction/gaiyou_kyoku/annai/midokoro/midokoro_jyuniko.html).

## Implementation order

1. **Rivers and their valleys.** Use an affordable formation-stage river model with related discharge, width, depth, bank migration and sediment behavior. Keep the existing catchment connections authoritative. Form the surrounding banks/floodplain with the channel; merely curving its water ribbon fails. Check confluences, coastal outlets, downhill stages, separated hollows and the physical support under every water edge. Existing straight-line and detached-pool failures remain acceptance failures.
2. **Lakes as a first-class landform, in the same hydrology pass.** Support real inland basins, including clusters of smaller forest lakes and larger valley lakes. First evaluate a bounded slope-failure/debris-dam or collapse process where geology and relief permit it; move rock/soil explicitly, preserve basin rims, resolve the spill outlet and fill from water supply. Separate tectonic and glacial basin families remain later formation extensions, not labels applied to arbitrary circles. Small basins need persistent sub-kilometre geometry because the 1 km world mesh cannot resolve them. Basin beds, water level, outlets and surrounding soil must survive save/reopen and agree in near/far sampling. Do not mistake wide channel patches or ocean cells for lakes.
3. **Falls, cascades, gorges and rock structure.** Preserve genuine knickpoints where a stream crosses resistant strata, a scarp or a basin outlet. Generate a supported lip, downstream drop and receiving channel/pool from the same bed and water state. Use existing geology to differentiate ledges, cliffs and weathering; avoid turning every voxel step into a waterfall. Couple exposed rock, slope debris and vegetation establishment to the resulting terrain.
4. **Findable mountain and hiking diversity.** Survey relief, cliff faces, lake basins and river features across representative seeds, then expose actual discovered sites in the atlas. Verify ground-level approaches and sustained views; a high-altitude mountain screenshot or a hidden exceptional seed is insufficient. Regional woodland, understory and small fauna continue alongside this work.

These systems must compose. A forest can contain a lake cluster, a steep outlet gorge and a viewpoint without changing biome. Mountains can include wooded foothills, bare outcrops, valleys and high basins. Use actual formative conditions and spatial scarcity; every small patch need not contain every landmark.

## Forest structure and density

The owner's [woodland-lake photograph](terrain-reconstruction/references/woodland-lake.png) is a visual reference supplied in this conversation. It shows overlapping crowns, branching and leaning trunks, smaller trees beneath mature trees, dense waterside vegetation and irregular openings. Exact species and ages are not inferred from the photograph. Its forest enclosure and layered structure are part of the desired experience, translated into the game's block-based visual style.

- Replace the current trunk-plus-small-crown-clusters appearance with coherent branching architecture. Species/form families, maturity, growing space, damage and local conditions should visibly change trunk thickness, lean, branch placement and crown shape. Merely labeling a tree with an age or resizing one model fails this requirement.
- Trunks, branches and leaves are solid metre-sized game blocks with collision, including leaf undersides and walkable canopy tops. Rendering and collision must evaluate the same deterministic block shape; camera teleports must avoid starting inside these solids. A hidden trunk cylinder or non-solid decorative crown does not satisfy this.
- True diversity must be visible: distinct height distributions, branch/fork architecture and crown proportions within and across families, with juveniles beside mature trees. Merely assigning ages to similarly sized models fails.
- Represent canopy trees, subcanopy trees, saplings, shrubs and ground plants together, with canopy gaps and patchy regeneration. Dense forest is not a uniformly spaced orchard or maximum tree count everywhere. Existing succession/competition must govern credible coverage; investigate why fresh revision-four worlds currently retain so little rooting soil/plant cover rather than bypassing that failure with decoration.
- Include appropriate ferns, reeds, moss/litter and deadwood in damp woodland and lake margins. Functional forms can come before an extensive species catalog, but keep their habitat and seasonal response explicit. Small fauna remains part of the requested breadth.
- Make light and silhouette support enclosure: distinguish shaded trunks/understory from exposed crowns and openings. Keep foliage undersides closed and textures stable during motion. Do not trade density for leaf shimmer, missing far forest or abrupt canopy disappearance.
- Budget richer trees through shared/instanced geometry and bounded nearby detail. Generate reproducible branching forms once, reuse them across instances, retain smaller forms only near the observer and preserve crown coverage with distant representations. Measure a dense lakeshore and a ground-level forest route against the rendering target; neither per-leaf full-world simulation nor unlimited baked branch geometry is required.
- Acceptance includes walking beneath a substantially closed canopy into a natural gap and down to a vegetated lake bank, with distinct mature/small tree forms and layered understory. Match the structural observations in the reference; no claim of photographic realism or exact reproduction is required.

## Budget and checks

- Keep the owner's 1,000 km world, metre blocks, approximately 10 km view, 240-second complete creation and 120-second terrain allocation. Rendering still targets actual 2560×1440 at smooth 120 FPS; that gate currently fails.
- Proposed added-formation allowance: at most **10 seconds for river refinement and 10 seconds for basin/landform events**, with measured total creation remaining the controlling limit. These are ceilings to test, not promises of current performance. Existing seed 99 already exceeds the terrain allocation, so recover that baseline cost rather than silently raising the limit.
- Perform expensive simulation on a coarse catchment or sparse bounded feature patch during generation. Persist its result and compile it into shared query data. Exploration should sample finished geography, with bounded workers and detail/vegetation streaming.
- Start with one river catchment and one forest-lake cluster, then check three representative world seeds. Retain failing controls. If a method fails its quality or cost test after a bounded investigation, record why and change the method; do not spend the entire project adjusting one family.
- A useful acceptance route is a continuous 2–5 km ground-level journey within one broad biome showing several distinct landforms and their transitions. Check at least a forest/lake route, a river/gorge route and a mountain route across the chosen seeds. This is a review sample, not a rule that places an attraction every fixed distance.
- Record actual lake count/area/depth, outlet connectivity, meaningful falls, local relief and cliff extent. Review the same locations in the game. Numeric presence alone does not pass the hiking-experience requirement.

## Long-term worldbuilding direction

After the realistic terrain foundation works well, expand toward an original Tolkien-like fantasy world with convincing large scale, fantasy biomes, creatures and broader worldbuilding. This is a major long-term direction, not a request to reproduce Middle-earth or add fantasy content before the natural foundation works.

Keep geography, geology, hydrology and ecology understandable enough that future authored or fantastic influences can change them coherently. Preserve space for exploration, discovery, useful resources, settlements and stories. Do not build a speculative fantasy framework now; add each system when its gameplay and worldbuilding requirements become concrete.
