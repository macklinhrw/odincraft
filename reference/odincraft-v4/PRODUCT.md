# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
Native macOS on Apple Silicon. Odin, SDL3 GPU with Metal, SDL_ttf; approved in the foundation plan.

## Users and purpose
Macklin explores a naturally formed block world in first person. Geographic causes should be inspectable with Dwarf Fortress-like information depth and modern readability. Colony simulation comes later.

## Confirmed constraints
Finite approximately 1,000 km world, substantial vertical depth, 10 km views, 120 FPS at 2560 × 1440, creation within four minutes. Tectonics, erosion, water, geology, climate, soil, plant ecosystems. No old project implementation is reused. Animals, civilizations, crafting and mining mechanics are deferred.

## Interface commitment
Restrained exploration and atlas interface, readable scalable text, real menus and keyboard/pointer interaction. Terrain is the main surface; detailed causes open on demand. The approved implementation request establishes the scope; this is not a new branding exercise.

## Evidence
The foundation plan records the owner's answers. Runtime reports describe only implemented milestones and measured workloads; absent measurements remain pending.
